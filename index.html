ABOUT XENON STUDIOS

Welcome to XENON STUDIOS: Where raw emotion becomes sound.

I'm meticulous and intentional in turning pure emotion into genuine, hard-hitting tracks where your vision as an artist meets the listener.

My approach combines the genuine authenticity and rawness of the classic productions of the 2000s, with modern and professional ear-candy. Above all, translating your vision from your head to the real world is my #1 goal.


Why choose me?


I'm not just here to record you, and this isn't a transactional relationship. I'm here as a creative partner. Special creative input, mentoring, and encouragement the entire process. Capturing your vision and emotion and building something you're truly proud of comes before everything else.
